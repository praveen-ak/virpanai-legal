Privacy Policy — Virpanai Connect

Last updated: 19 April 2026

Virpanai Connect ("we," "our," or "us") provides a mobile app building service for online stores. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

When you install and use Virpanai Connect, we collect the following data from your connected store:

  • Store information: store name, domain, email, and owner details
  • Products: details, variants, images, pricing, and inventory
  • Collections and categories
  • Orders: order details, addresses, status, and fulfillment info
  • Customers: names, emails, phone numbers, and addresses
  • App configuration: settings and customizations you create

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Sync your store data to the mobile application
  • Build, customize, and deploy your mobile app
  • Display products and content to your app users
  • Process orders placed through the mobile app
  • Send service-related notifications and support communications
  • Improve the performance and reliability of our service

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We may share data only with:

  • Service providers (hosting, analytics, push notifications) under confidentiality
  • The Apple App Store and Google Play to publish your app under your accounts
  • Legal authorities when required by law

4. Data Retention

We retain your data while your account is active. If you uninstall the app, we delete your data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), secure storage, and strict access controls.

6. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data by contacting us at support@getvirpanai.in. You can uninstall the app at any time to stop further data collection.

7. GDPR and CCPA Compliance

If you are in the EU, UK, or California, you have additional rights including data portability, the right to object, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. We respond to verified requests within 30 days.

8. Cookies

Our dashboard uses essential cookies to maintain your login session. We do not use third-party tracking cookies.

9. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed at individuals under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified by email or through the dashboard.

11. Contact Us

Website: https://getvirpanai.in
